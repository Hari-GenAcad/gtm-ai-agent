# LaunchPad product brief

LaunchPad is a campaign workspace for revenue teams. It turns an approved product brief into coordinated LinkedIn, email, blog, and advertising drafts while retaining citations to the supplied brief.

LaunchPad is intended for product marketing managers and revenue leaders who need a consistent starting point for campaign editing. It does not publish content to external platforms.

The local preview launches on November 1, 2026. Introductory pricing is INR 4,999 per workspace per month. The product requires human editing and approval before any content is published.
