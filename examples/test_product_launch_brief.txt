LaunchPilot AI - Approved Product Launch Brief

Product overview
LaunchPilot AI is a campaign planning workspace for B2B SaaS product marketing teams. It brings approved product facts, launch milestones, audience notes, and campaign drafts into one workflow.

Approved product capabilities
- Imports PDF, Markdown, CSV, XLSX, and Notion export files.
- Retrieves relevant source passages before generating content.
- Produces a LinkedIn post, promotional email, short blog draft, and three ad variations.
- Reviews content for factual grounding, tone, audience fit, and consistency.
- Sends unresolved claims to a human reviewer instead of inventing facts.

Target audience
The primary audience is product marketing managers and startup founders at B2B SaaS companies with 10 to 200 employees. A secondary audience is revenue leaders who coordinate launches across product, sales, and marketing teams.

Customer problem
Launch information is often spread across briefs, calendars, spreadsheets, and old campaign documents. This creates inconsistent messaging, missed dates, and unsupported claims.

Value proposition
LaunchPilot AI turns approved launch information into a coordinated, ready-to-edit campaign suite while preserving source attribution and human control.

Approved messaging
- Plan once and launch consistently across every channel.
- Turn approved product evidence into coordinated campaign content.
- Keep human reviewers in control of risky or incomplete claims.

Tone guidance
Use a clear, confident, practical, and evidence-led tone. Avoid hype, guarantees, competitor comparisons, and claims such as best, revolutionary, or risk-free.

Required factual boundaries
- Do not claim that LaunchPilot AI publishes content automatically.
- Do not claim support for private Google Drive or Notion accounts.
- Do not promise revenue growth or guaranteed campaign performance.
- If a fact is not present in this brief, identify it as missing rather than inventing it.

Past approved campaign line
Your launch facts are already written down. LaunchPilot AI helps your team turn them into one consistent campaign.
